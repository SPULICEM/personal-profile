Postdoctoral researcher
Cemaden / MCTI, funded by FAPESP
São José dos Campos, Brazil

I am an environmental researcher focused on interdisciplinary studies in multiple areas. I aim to incorporate a policy process perspective in all my research by combining theoretical and analytical tools from different fields to offer diverse perspectives on complex problems.

I am currently interested in how societies and policy systems may change in the context of climate crises and vulnerabilities. My recent work seeks to understand how ideas and beliefs may shape collective action, how power relations between actors inform policy and to what extent these processes may exacerbate Global South problems.

Main areas of interest

Interdisciplinary research bridging environmental and policy process and policy analysis sciences.

Transition to sustainability

How power relations and asymmetries shape sociotechnical development, particularly in the Global South context.

Technological legitimacy, power coalitions and discourse lock-in in Brazil’s wind energy transition, studied through discourse network analysis.

Climate policy and governance

How coalitions and decision-maker actors inform the policy process from a network perspective in the context of the climate crisis.

Climate policy networks to examine collaborations, information exchange and influence in the Brazilian climate context.

Water, food and energy

How the interconnected dimensions can be understood by policymakers.

Water–energy–food nexus, collaborative sustainability indices aligned with the 2030 Agenda, and actor networks in socio-ecological water governance.

Disaster risk reduction

Helping local governments and communities develop better processes for the prevention of floods, droughts and other extreme weather, with warnings built centred on people.

Organisational preparedness capacities for extreme weather and climate events, using mixed-methods action research.

Resilient cities

Practical ways for cities to cut emissions and protect people and infrastructure from climate impacts, as well as improving governance capacity toward a more resilient urban state.

Urban decarbonisation, climate resilience and public-policy capacity within the CCD Carbon Neutral Cities centre.

Measuring socioeconomic impacts from territorial interventions

Assessing impact through the application of quantitative analysis in infrastructure projects.

Municipal human development, financial compensation and benefit-sharing mechanisms in the impact assessment of Brazilian hydropower.

Active projects

Current research, from disaster to conservation governance.

An applied research centre helping cities fight climate change through emission reduction, nature-based solutions and climate resilience, working with companies, governments and institutions across urban development, mobility, buildings, renewables and sanitation.

2023 – present

COPE: Organisational Preparedness for Extreme Events

Studies how local governments and communities prepare for extreme weather and climate events, and co-produces ways to strengthen public policy. Uses surveys, interviews, focus groups and participatory workshops, with attention to age, gender and disability. Linked to SDGs 11 and 13.

FAPESP grant 22/02891-9 (BV FAPESP)

2022 – present

BridgingVALUES

A transdisciplinary project on how EU and global conservation policies interact with local governance of protected areas, and how this affects biodiversity, ecosystem services and social equity. Six case studies across Europe and the Global South; the Brazilian final scientific report was delivered in 2026.
